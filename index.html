01 — About

Between the clinic and the sequencer

I am a medical doctor and researcher working in an oncology clinic, where I assist in patient management and chemotherapy planning. Alongside the clinic, I identify non-coding RNAs that work as diagnostic and prognostic biomarkers in human tumours.

My training spans cancer biology, molecular genetics and bioinformatics — bulk RNA-seq and single-cell analysis in particular. What holds those threads together is a preference for questions that reach a patient: a marker that shortens time to diagnosis, or an explanation for why one regimen fails where another holds.

Outside medicine I directed theatre for three years at my university, which taught me more about running a research group than any methods course did.

02 — Research

Focus & methods

  • Non-coding RNA biomarkers

    miRNAs and lncRNAs as diagnostic and prognostic markers across colorectal, renal, gastric, ovarian and breast tumours.

  • Therapy resistance

    Mechanisms behind cisplatin, paclitaxel, doxorubicin, gemcitabine and TKI resistance — PI3K/AKT, MAPK, WNT, NOTCH and forkhead regulation.

  • Bioinformatics

    Bulk RNA-seq and single-cell workflows: QC, integration, clustering, differential expression and cell–cell interaction in the tumour microenvironment.

  • Wet lab

    RNA/DNA extraction, cDNA synthesis, real-time PCR, cell culture, western blot, MTT assay, biobank design and curation.

  • Analysis & tooling

    R (advanced), Linux, SPSS. Systematic review and meta-analysis methodology.

  • Clinical oncology

    Patient management, chemotherapy planning, evidence-based practice and multidisciplinary tumour board collaboration.
